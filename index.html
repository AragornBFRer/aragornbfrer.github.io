I study how to make reliable decisions with imperfect models, with a focus on uncertainty quantification, conformal inference, and causal inference. More recently, I have been working on multi-agent AI systems for scientific discovery and the principled evaluation of AI systems.

I am a PhD student in Statistics at the Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania, and collaborate closely with Prof. Ying Jin. Before that, I studied Mathematics and Computer Science at HKUST, where I worked on conformal prediction with Prof. Xinzhou Guo and on cognitive science and intention understanding with Prof. Yangqiu Song at the KnowComp Group. In Spring 2025, I visited ETH Zurich.

During my undergrad, I also did internships in quantitative research at Hangzhou Wangdao, hedge fund research at Jihai Investment, and software development at Hundsun.
